Trend signals

Algorithmic technical-analysis signals — trend vs. the 50-day average, RSI, and MACD — computed from price history, recomputed every few minutes. Not financial advice or a recommendation. These describe momentum/trend patterns, not predictions; you decide every trade yourself.

Intraday pulse

Same technical read as Trend signals, but computed from 5-minute bars (last few trading sessions) instead of daily bars, recomputed roughly every 5 minutes — a closer-in read on what's happening right now. Not financial advice or a recommendation.

Focus ticker · AMC

Same IEX stock feed and indicative options feed as the rest of the dashboard, at finer granularity for one symbol at a time.

Intraday technicals

Finer-grained technical read for the focus ticker — RSI(14) on 1-minute and 5-minute bars, distance from session VWAP, and a volume-surge flag, recomputed roughly every minute. Not financial advice or a recommendation. These describe momentum/trend patterns, not predictions; you decide every trade yourself.

Nearest weekly options · near the money

Alpaca's free "indicative" options feed (approximate, not true real-time OPRA). Sorted by volume; vol > OI flags strikes where today's volume exceeds open interest — a common heuristic for unusual activity, not a buy/sell signal.
